# DMI Design Rules

This file contains project-specific DMI conventions. Update it as validated DMI design-system and product rules are discovered.

## Current known product context
The DMI Dashboard requires differentiated homepage experiences for New-to-Bank (NTB) and Rejected users.

### NTB
- Keep Personal Loan and Business Loan prominent.
- Highlight Credit Score.
- Highlight PFM.
- Include Bill Payments & Recharges.
- Include Wealth product information nudge.

### Rejected
- Hide Apply for Personal Loan.
- Make PFM the primary service.
- Credit Score is secondary.
- Bill Payments & Recharges are secondary.
- Include Wealth product information nudge.

## Important product principle
Recommendations should connect UX changes to funnel behaviour and measurable business outcomes.
