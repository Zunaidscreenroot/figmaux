# UX Audit

Use for auditing Figma screens, flows, wireframes, or screenshots.

## Objective
Evaluate the experience from both UX and business perspectives.

## Audit areas
1. User and intent
2. Information architecture
3. Navigation and discoverability
4. Interaction and CTA hierarchy
5. Visual hierarchy
6. Cognitive load
7. Feedback and error prevention
8. Accessibility
9. Consistency with the existing design system
10. Personalisation and user state
11. Empty, loading, error, and edge states
12. Funnel and business impact

## Issue format
For each meaningful issue provide:
- Issue
- Severity: Critical / High / Medium / Low
- Evidence
- User impact
- Business impact
- Recommendation
- Expected KPI impact

Do not modify the design during an audit unless explicitly requested.
