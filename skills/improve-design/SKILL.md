# Improve Design

Use after an audit or when the designer requests design improvements.

## Process
1. Review the current design and relevant audit findings.
2. Group recommendations into P0, P1, and P2.
3. Explain the expected UX and business impact.
4. Wait for approval before implementing significant changes.
5. Create a new iteration rather than overwriting the approved/current design unless explicitly instructed.
6. Re-audit after implementation.

## Priority
- P0: critical usability, business, accessibility, or funnel issues.
- P1: high-impact improvements.
- P2: useful polish or optimisation opportunities.
