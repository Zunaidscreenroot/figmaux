# Business Analysis

Analyse designs through product, funnel, and business outcomes.

For each important design element or recommendation determine:
1. User behaviour encouraged
2. Funnel step affected
3. Product outcome
4. Business outcome
5. KPI that can measure the impact

Use:
Design change -> User behaviour -> Funnel behaviour -> Product outcome -> Business KPI

Consider conversion, engagement, retention, revenue, cross-sell, data collection, and future underwriting value where relevant.

Prioritise:
1. Business impact
2. User impact
3. Implementation effort

Do not claim numerical improvements without supporting data. Use directional language when evidence is unavailable.
